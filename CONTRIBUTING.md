# Contributing to KAI AI Removes

Thank you for improving the project.

## Principles

- Keep media processing browser-local.
- Do not add tracking or silent uploads.
- Clearly disclose technical limitations.
- Keep responsible-use confirmations visible.
- Keep the **KAI AI** creator credit and LinkedIn attribution visible.
- Test image and video workflows in a current Chromium-based browser before submitting changes.
